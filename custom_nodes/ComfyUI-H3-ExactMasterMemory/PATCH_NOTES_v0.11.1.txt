ComfyUI-H3-ExactMasterMemory v0.11.1 (Reference Director 01 v2.1)

H3RDHQGate, H3RDMotionGate, H3RDFaceGate
- New optional VIDEO input "run_after". Connect the normal video (CREATE FINAL VIDEO) to it: the
  group then starts only after the normal video is created, and ComfyUI saves and previews the
  normal video first, so you can review it while the upscale, motion or face pass runs.
  Without it, ComfyUI may run an optional group before saving the normal video.
- Nothing else changes; workflows without the link behave as before.

Installers
- COMPATIBLE_WITH also names 0.11.0.
