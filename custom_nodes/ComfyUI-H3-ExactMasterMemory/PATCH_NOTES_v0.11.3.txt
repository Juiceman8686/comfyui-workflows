ComfyUI-H3-ExactMasterMemory v0.11.3 (Reference Director 01 v2.1.3)

H3RDKeepSourceColors (new)  "H3 Keep Source Colors - Upscale Colour Lock (v2)"
- Gives an upscaled / refined video the colours of the normal video and keeps the upscale's detail.
  Inputs: images (the upscaled frames), source_images (the normal video's frames, any size: they are
  resized to the upscaled size), chroma_from_source (1.0 = every colour from the normal video; 0 =
  off), max_kept_shift (0 = strict; 4-6 keeps the refine's small colour nuances, measured at about
  2/255 on skin, and removes only larger changes; a faint trace of a strong recolouring can
  remain), brightness_from_source (0 = off; 0.3-0.5 also pulls the coarse brightness toward the normal
  video while fine detail stays), coarse_px, frames_per_batch.
- Why: the HQ refine starts at 75 % noise (shift 12, denoise 0.20). It keeps the shapes of the
  upscaled latent but re-decides the colour of thin bright details: on a rainy car scene the wet
  hair strands beside the face came out teal-green in the upscaled video while the normal video had
  them black. Frame statistics show no global colour shift and no tiling seams; the green follows
  single strands. Colours are not what an upscale is for, so the finished upscaled frames now take
  their chroma from the normal video (BT.709 luma / chroma split) and keep only the upscale's
  brightness detail. Nothing in the sampling changes.
- Frame counts must match: connect the source before the exact-duration trim (BACKEND - VIDEO
  DECODE), as workflow v2.1.3 does. A single source frame is broadcast. Frames are processed in
  small batches on the device of the input.
- The report line names the frame count, the percentages and whether the source was resized.
