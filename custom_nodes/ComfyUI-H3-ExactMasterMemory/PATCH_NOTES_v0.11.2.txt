ComfyUI-H3-ExactMasterMemory v0.11.2 (Reference Director 01 v2.1.1)

H3RDLoraStack
- New optional field "look_loras_below_noise" (last field; default 1.0 = the v2.1 behaviour, so
  workflows and API prompts saved with v0.11.1 keep every value and validate). Lowest value 0.15,
  at which only the final step (which produces noise 0) carries the look LoRAs.
- Below 1.0, the look LoRAs (Realism People, LMS Sharpness, custom 1, custom 2) run only on the
  sampling steps that PRODUCE a noise level below this value. A step is judged by the noise it
  produces (the next entry of the sampler's schedule): it is OFF when that noise is at or above the
  value, fades in over 0.10 of noise, and runs at full strength from (value - 0.10) down. The early
  steps, which decide framing, body structure and motion, run the plain model. Motion Continuity
  Repair and Spatial Physics are never windowed.
- Why: with H3's flow shift of 12, the v2.1 realism schedule (beta 20 + ExtendIntermediateSigmas
  below 0.7 = 24 steps) spends its first 14 steps entirely above noise 0.75. In v2.1 the Realism
  People LoRA ran on all of them too; close-ups were fine, but on medium and full-body shots its
  portrait bias drifted the framing, the body and the motion. Shapes and skin form in the late
  steps; the window keeps the LoRA there. 0.75 is the skin band of H3 Veda Settings.
- What 0.75 does on the shipped schedules (shift 12):
    Speed Mode OFF, 24 steps:  off on steps 1-14, fading in on step 15, full on steps 16-24.
    TURBO 8-step (simple):     off on steps 1-6, full on the last 2.
    TURBO 4-step (simple):     off on steps 1-3, full on the last step (which goes straight to 0).
  So in the TURBO modes the look LoRAs act only on the last step or two. Set 1.0 for turbo drafts
  if you want the v2.1 turbo look.
- The refine passes do not start low with shift 12: the upscale refine (simple, 8 steps at denoise
  0.20) starts at noise 0.75 and the face refine (8 steps at 0.40) at 0.89. The REFINE stack of
  workflow v2.1.1 therefore ships with 1.0 (window off). A window there would remove the look
  LoRAs from the first face-refine steps.
- Runtime mode only (the LoRA strength is changed per step through the bypass hooks). In merged
  mode the field is ignored and the report says so. Needs a ComfyUI core with APPLY_MODEL wrappers
  (any core new enough for runtime LoRAs has them); otherwise the report says the window is off.
  A LoRA's non-adapter patches (bias / weight diffs), if any, are merged at full strength and are
  not windowed; the report marks them.
- The report names the window and marks every LoRA that runs inside it. When the render starts,
  the console prints how many steps of the real schedule carry the look LoRAs, for example:
  "[H3RDLoraStack] look window 0.75: 24 steps; the look LoRAs run on the last 9 (+1 fading in)
  and are off on the first 14."
- The noise level is the sampler's sigma (1 = pure noise, 0 = finished), the same scale as
  skin_band_below_noise on H3 Veda Settings.

Installers
